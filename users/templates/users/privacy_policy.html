Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 12, 2026 Version 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to Live More. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our mobile application (the "Live More App") and related backend services.

By downloading, accessing, or using the Live More App, you consent to the collection, transfer, storage, disclosure, and other uses of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal and health-related information to provide you with personalized fitness calculations, sync capability, and analytics. The types of data we collect include:

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Data: When you sign up, we collect your email address, full name, and password credentials to securely manage your account and authentication.
  • Onboarding Profile Metrics: To accurately calculate your health metrics, we collect your age, sex/gender, height, current weight, and goal weight.

B. Device & Sensor Data (via Bluetooth BLE Pod)

Our companion hardware pod collects and transmits the following metrics to the Live More App over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE):

  • Heart Rate (BPM): Real-time and average heart rate readings during rest and workouts.
  • Step Count & Cadence: Steps taken and speed of steps to measure active movements.
  • Movement/Activity Levels: Accelerometer data to infer exercise intensity.
  • Workout Duration: Time spent during active workout sessions.

C. Technical App Data

  • Device Metrics: Connection status, battery level of the companion pod, IP address, and user agent strings collected during sync operations.
  • Security Log History: Timestamps of logins and access points for authentication security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our services, and compliance with our legal obligations. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Estimate Calories Burned: Calculate active and resting calories using your profile metrics (Age, Sex, Height, Weight) combined with your Heart Rate and Step Count via the BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) and MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) formulas.
  • Track and Display Fitness Progress: Store and visualize your health data, workouts, and weight logs across daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly charts on your dashboard.
  • Manage Accounts: Authenticate users, process registration OTPs, perform password changes, and verify active sessions.
  • Improve App Performance: Analyze device sync behaviors, battery trends, and server logs to optimize app stability.
  • Provide Support: Respond to your technical inquiries, bugs, and support tickets submitted through the app.

4. Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. All information collected is stored securely on our backend servers hosted on high-performance Virtual Private Servers (VPS). We implement robust technical measures, including:

  • Encryption: Data is transmitted securely using industry-standard SSL/HTTPS protocols. User passwords are encrypted using secure cryptographic hashing algorithms.
  • Access Controls: Restrictive server configurations and authorization policies ensuring that only you (via your Bearer JWT token) can access your health metrics.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You have complete control over your data and may request deletion at any time:

Self-Service Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated health data (including weight logs, workouts, step counts, and heart rates) directly in the app settings, or by visiting our public data deletion portal at the following endpoints:

  • Delete Entire Account: Submits an immediate deletion of your profile, email, credentials, and health metrics from our system.
  • Delete Profile Data: Anonymizes or clears all health logs while leaving the basic account shell intact.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Live More App backend does not use cookies for tracking purposes. We use stateless JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored locally on your device to maintain your authenticated login session. These tokens expire automatically and do not track your browsing activity across third-party websites.

7. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your health, fitness, or personal information to outside parties. Your fitness measurements are computed locally and stored on our private backend instance without sharing with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this data from our servers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
